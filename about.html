About TravelMate

Built for Smart & Stress-Free Travel

Empowering travelers, backpackers, and group adventurers to plan itineraries and split expenses effortlessly.

Our Mission

TravelMate was created to eliminate the awkwardness and mathematical chaos of group travel budgeting. Whether you are backpacking through Himachal, embarking on a coastal road trip in Goa with friends, or organizing a weekend getaway, TravelMate provides accurate expense splitting, fair share calculations, and smart destination budgeting.

Built with a high-performance, lightweight architecture using pure HTML5, modern CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, TravelMate is lightning fast, 100% private, and works offline right inside your browser without requiring server logins or cloud databases.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

💡 How does the debt settlement algorithm work?

TravelMate calculates each member's total expenditure and determines who overpaid and who underpaid relative to their equal share. It then applies a Greedy minimum cash-flow optimization to settle all debts with the absolute minimum number of payment transfers.

📱 Does TravelMate work without internet?

Yes! TravelMate is designed as an offline-first client application. All trips, expenses, and itinerary data are stored securely on your device in your browser's LocalStorage.

📊 Can I export my trip records to Excel or PDF?

Absolutely. You can download an instant CSV spreadsheet formatted for Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, or print a formatted PDF expense summary invoice directly from the Trip Expenses page.
