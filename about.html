Project Overview

About TravelMate

A simple, lightweight travel planning companion crafted for college students, solo adventurers, and group travelers.

Our Purpose

TravelMate is a mini travel-planning project designed to help users explore destinations and estimate travel expenses. Whether you are planning a solo weekend retreat to the hills of Manali or an exciting group beach holiday to Goa, TravelMate takes away the confusion of budgeting by providing clear, customizable cost projections.

This project was developed with a clean, beginner-friendly architecture using pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. It requires no complex backend setups, no databases, and no login portals—simply open the website in your browser to explore places and calculate your trip budget accurately.

🌐 HTML5 Semantic Layout 🎨 Modern CSS3 & Variables ⚡ Vanilla JavaScript 📱 Mobile Responsive 🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy
